The people (and cats) we love

Our Family

E

Eliza

she/her

Eliza is a woman who embraces her nerdiness. A full-time programmer and dungeon master extraordinaire, she delights in solving problems and creating immersive worlds for her friends. She loves to read, with her favorite genres being romance and science fiction. She also loves to cook! Ask her about her latest culinary adventures, and she is sure to give you an earfull.

M

Malone

they/them

Malone is a non-binary person who loves a good craft almost as much as a good book! A passionate hobbyist and lifelong learner, Malone is working towards their Master's in Education while working as a receptionist at MSU and running their own craft business on the side! They like to spend their free time by enjoying cozy indie games like Sticky Business and Stardew Valley.

Our favorite little companions

D

Doorknob

At 5 years old, Doorknob is a curious and cuddly cat who loves to explore every corner of the house. The most affectionate of the trio, he is also the one who gets into the most trouble. He is known for his love of hugs and his habit of sitting directly in front of the TV, so as not to feel left out.

B

Bagheera

Bagheera is a large and majestic cat who loves to observe the world from a distance. He has been Malone's closest companion for 13 years. He is known for his size, handsome appearance, and his ability to brighten any bad day with a meow and a headbutt.

M

Molly

Molly is the newest addition to the family, but the oldest cat. After requesting entrance to the household almost a year ago to flee a storm, she quickly made her presence known with her loud and opinionated personality. She is estimated to be more than 14 years old, making her the matriarch of the cat colony.
